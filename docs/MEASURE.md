# Measurements from Jon's gear

Phase 1 needs real numbers. A kitchen scale (grams) and a ruler or calipers (mm) are enough.
Write each number in the table; leave a blank if it isn't easy to get.

**Photos work too.** Put a ruler or tape measure right next to the part, shoot straight on (not at
an angle), and drop the photos in `C:\dev\custom-skate\measure\`. Claude can read lengths off
them. Calipers are better for anything under ~5 mm (wall thickness).

Jon has both Marsblade roller frames in Medium, on different skates. The **O1 Medium on his True
TF9 size 6 fits**: that's the design target. The R1 Medium (on a size 7 Tour boot) is the weight
and rocker to beat.

## Your feet, with a ruler (can be done alone)

Until the scans exist, these scale the stand-in scan to your feet. Stand on a sheet of paper with
your heel against a wall, weight on that foot, and mark the tip of your longest toe and both
sides of the widest part (the ball). Both feet.
